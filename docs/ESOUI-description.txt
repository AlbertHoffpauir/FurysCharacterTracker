[CENTER][SIZE="6"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Fury's Character Tracker[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[SIZE="3"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][I]Every character on your account, on one board.[/I][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]
[CENTER][SIZE="1"][COLOR="#B4B0D8"]FURY'S ADDONS[/COLOR]   [URL="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info5063.html"]Guild Toolset[/URL]   |   [COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Character Tracker[/B][/COLOR]   |   [URL="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info5099.html"]Inventory Ledger[/URL]   |   [URL="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info5106.html"]Account Sync[/URL]   |   [URL="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info5115.html"]Quality of Life[/URL][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[B]Your whole roster at a glance.[/B] Skill and class lines, crafting, research, daily writs, skill points still to earn, wallet, worn gear, companions and progress over time, read straight from the game for every character you have. The Daily and Weekly tabs tell you who still has something to do. Put your best numbers on a brag card, paste a tab into Discord, and take it all to Excel.
[QUOTE][SIZE="3"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]New here? Start here[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
Log in on each character once. After that the board keeps itself up to date while you play, and the [B]Start here[/B] page explains everything on it in one screen: the colors, the tabs, the alert switches. The [B]Daily[/B] tab then shows what every character can still do today, and the [B]Weekly[/B] tab your score and place on the weekly leaderboards.[/QUOTE]
[LIST]
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Everything starts turned off.[/B][/COLOR]  The chat alerts, the login summary and opening the board at login are all off until you turn them on, with one click on the Start here page or in the settings. Updating keeps your settings as they were.
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Read only.[/B][/COLOR]  It reads what the game shows and never changes a character. Roles, gear picks and notes are your own; Wearing shows what a character had on when it was last read.
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Light.[/B][/COLOR]  After the first read it reads only what changed, never during combat, and a burst of changes counts as one.
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Any client language.[/B][/COLOR]  Skill lines, crafting writs and companion quests are matched by the game's language-neutral IDs.
[/LIST]
[B]Requires:[/B] [URL="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info7-LibAddonMenu.html"]LibAddonMenu-2.0[/URL] (version 43 or newer). Install it separately, from the link or by searching for LibAddonMenu in Minion. Without it, this addon will not load.

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Getting started[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*]Install [B]LibAddonMenu-2.0[/B] (see Requires above).
[*]Log in on each character once with the addon on. A few seconds is enough: it is read right after login, and again by itself whenever you make progress.
[*]Type [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct[/B][/FONT] (or bind a key under Controls > Keybindings > Fury's Character Tracker) to open the board. Characters not read yet are the dim rows at the bottom.
[*]Click [B]Start here[/B] on the board (or [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct start[/B][/FONT]) for one page on how it all works. It opens by itself the first time, and the alert switches are on it.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]The board[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I]One row per character, one tab per subject.[/I][/COLOR]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Reading it[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*][B]Violet tile[/B] = maxed, a [B]bar[/B] behind a number = how far along, dim dash = not started, [B]blue[/B] = your own pick. Easy to read by brightness alone, not just color.
[*]Hover any cell, column title, tab or button for what it means.
[*]Click a column title to sort, again to reverse, a third time for your own order.
[*]Click a character for everything saved about it: a daily checklist, picks, class lines, every skill line, skill points still to earn, research with timers and every missing trait, worn gear and progress.
[*]Right-click a row for its menu: details, move up or down, note, copy for Discord, remove.
[*]The bottom row counts how many characters have each column maxed.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]The tabs[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*][B]Overview:[/B] how many skill lines of each kind are maxed, with skill points, research, companions, riding, achievement points and hours played.
[*][B]Class:[/B] level, race, alliance and the three class skill lines each character is using, with subclassing. Other class lines it has trained are listed beside them.
[*][B]Weapons, Armor, Guilds & AvA, World, Crafting:[/B] the rank of every skill line.
[*][B]Research:[/B] traits known per craft, research slots in use, and the time until the next one finishes. Hover a craft for what is researching and every missing trait.
[*][B]Daily:[/B] what each character can still do today. Crafting writs since today's reset (started, ready to hand in, done), the next riding lesson, free research slots, and the first random dungeon, battleground and Tales of Tribute match of the day (reward and large XP bonus). The line above shows your daily and weekly Challenges.
[*][B]Weekly:[/B] the weekly leaderboards. The first two columns are this week's trial (some weeks a group arena) and this week's arena challenge, and their titles are the names the game gives them. Then Infinite Archive (solo and duo), battlegrounds, and the reward tier earned in the Cyrodiil home campaign. Each row shows which characters ran what this week. The bottom row is the account's best score and place on each leaderboard: a leaderboard keeps one entry per account, and its rewards go to the top 500. Amber = a score outside the top 500 or with no place, so you know which run to redo. The line above counts down to the reset and lists what needs a better run. Everything empties by itself when the week turns over.
[*][B]Collections:[/B] recipes, furnishing plans, motifs, scribing, riding, companions and time played.
[*][B]Skill points:[/B] where the points still to earn are. Skyshards (hover for the zones that still have some), public dungeon group events (hover for the ones still to do) and Alliance War rank.
[*][B]Wallet & CP:[/B] gold, Tel Var, Alliance Points, writ vouchers and bag space per character with totals, Champion Points per tree, and on the line above the bank and what the whole account shares.
[*][B]Progress:[/B] what each character gained in the last 7 and 30 days: gold, hours played, skill points, achievement points and traits.
[*][B]Roles & gear:[/B] click a blue cell to pick a role, gear and a planned race, write a note, and see the sets each character is wearing.
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct class[/B][/FONT] (or any tab name; [FONT="Courier New"][B]traits[/B][/FONT] for Research, [FONT="Courier New"][B]points[/B][/FONT] for Skill points, [FONT="Courier New"][B]sets[/B][/FONT] for Roles & gear) opens straight to a tab.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Research timers[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]Every research in progress, one line each, with time left and when it finishes, soonest first. Finished and free slots are at the top in amber. Button under the board, [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct timers[/B][/FONT], or a key.
[*]Timers count down for every character, not just the one you are on.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Copy for Discord[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]The tab you are looking at as text, ready to paste. It opens selected: press Ctrl+C, then paste. For one character, right-click its row.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Alerts[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]A chat line when research finishes or a riding lesson is ready on any character, even ones on other accounts, and a one-line summary at login. They start turned off: switch each one on the Start here page or in the settings, or use [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct alerts on[/B][/FONT] | [FONT="Courier New"][B]off[/B][/FONT].
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Other accounts and megaservers[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]The button at the top right picks which account and megaserver the board shows. Every account that plays on the same PC saves into the same file, so each one shows up after it logs in once with the addon on. Another account shows what it saved at its last logout.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Brag card[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I][FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct brag[/B][/FONT][/I][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]One screen of your best numbers, sized for a screenshot to share on Discord: hours played, Champion Points, skill points, skill lines maxed, traits researched, Grandmaster crafters, Vampire + Werewolf, scribing, companions, riding, and the character with the most achievement points, recipes, furnishing plans and motif styles.
[*]Set items collected (sticker book) and outfit styles too. Tiles with a finish line show % complete and a progress bar.
[*]A strip at the bottom shows which roles you play on every class.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Windows Tools[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I]Optional, separate download. The addon works fully in game without them.[/I][/COLOR]
The offline viewer and the Excel export run outside the game, so ESOUI's rules keep them out of the addon zip. Get them here: [B][URL="https://github.com/AlbertHoffpauir/FurysCharacterTracker/raw/main/downloads/FurysCharacterTracker-WindowsTools-1.8.4.zip"]Fury's Character Tracker Windows Tools 1.8.4[/URL][/B], and unzip them into any folder you like, for example Documents\Elder Scrolls Online. In game, the [B]Get the Windows Tools[/B] button in the settings (or [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct tools[/B][/FONT]) opens the same download page in your browser.
[B]Updating from 1.4.1 or older?[/B] Get the new tools too. The older tools cannot read what addon 1.5.0 saves.
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Offline viewer[/B][/COLOR]
The same board in your web browser with the game closed, for every account and megaserver on your PC: every tab, Start here, research timers, the brag card, character details and Copy for Discord. It runs the addon's own board files, so it shows exactly what the game shows.
[LIST=1]
[*]Log out, or type [FONT="Courier New"][B]/reloadui[/B][/FONT]. The game only writes its saved data at those moments.
[*]Double-click [B]Open Offline Viewer.cmd[/B] in the tools folder. Your browser opens [B]Fury's Character Tracker.html[/B], which it also saves in Documents\Elder Scrolls Online.
[*]To see newer data later, run it again. Picks, notes and your row order are changed in game.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Export to Excel[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*][B]The quick way:[/B] open the offline viewer, pick the account, click [B]Save for Excel[/B]. No reload in game is needed.
[*][B]Written for you:[/B] double-click [B]Turn On Auto Export.cmd[/B] once. A small helper runs in the background (no admin rights needed). After that, click [B]Export to Excel[/B] on the board (or [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct export[/B][/FONT]): the UI reloads, and a few seconds later [B]ESO Characters.csv[/B] is in Documents\Elder Scrolls Online, with one row per character for every account. If it is open in Excel, you get a dated copy beside it.
[*][B]Turn Off Auto Export.cmd[/B] stops the helper; run it before you delete the tools folder. Without the helper, run [B]ExportToExcel.cmd[/B] yourself.
[/LIST]
[B]What each tool file does[/B]
[SPOILER]
The scripts are plain text (batch, PowerShell) you can open in Notepad. Full source: [URL="https://github.com/AlbertHoffpauir/FurysCharacterTracker/tree/main/WindowsTools"]WindowsTools on GitHub[/URL]. They only read the addon's saved data file (FurysCharacterTracker.lua in the SavedVariables folder of live for NA and liveeu for EU) and never connect to the internet or touch the game's files.
[LIST]
[*][B]Open Offline Viewer.cmd / OfflineViewer.ps1:[/B] puts the saved data into a copy of Viewer\viewer.html, saves it as Fury's Character Tracker.html in Documents\Elder Scrolls Online and opens it.
[*][B]Viewer\viewer.html:[/B] the page. It holds a copy of the addon's board files and Fengari, an open-source Lua engine (MIT license), to run them in your browser. It loads nothing from the internet.
[*][B]ExportToExcel.cmd / ExportToExcel.ps1:[/B] turns the addon's export rows into a CSV in Documents\Elder Scrolls Online.
[*][B]Turn On Auto Export.cmd:[/B] adds one small .vbs to your Windows Startup folder that starts AutoExport.ps1 hidden at sign-in, and starts it now. [B]Turn Off Auto Export.cmd[/B] removes it and stops the helper.
[*][B]AutoExport.ps1:[/B] every 3 seconds checks whether the saved data changed; when it holds a new export request, runs ExportToExcel.ps1. Keeps a small log and state file in the tools folder.
[/LIST]
[/SPOILER]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Notes on the numbers[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]A character only updates when you log in on it. Research and riding timers keep counting for all of them.
[*][B]Skill points[/B] are counted the way the game counts them: subclass skills cost double. Max 586.
[*][B]Vampire / Werewolf:[/B] the game hides these lines when you are cured, so the board keeps the best rank it has ever seen.
[*][B]Companions:[/B] the collectible is account-wide, but each character has to finish the companion's first quest. A companion counts once that quest is done on that character, or once you've summoned it there. Level and rapport are saved for a companion while it is out.
[*][B]Wearing[/B] is what the character had on when it was last read. [B]Trials[/B] counts trial quests handed in during the last 7 days. [B]Progress[/B] starts counting the day you install 1.5.0.
[*][B]Scribing[/B] is grimoires + scripts known. [B]Motifs[/B] are styles known in full, the game's own count. [B]Recipes[/B] and [B]furnishing plans[/B] use the game's own lists.
[*]A renamed character keeps its row.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Questions[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[B]I just updated. Why do some columns show a dash?[/B]
The new columns fill in when a character is read by this version. Log in on each character once, a few seconds is enough. The line above the board says how many are left, and [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct unread[/B][/FONT] names them.

[B]I play two accounts at once (two game clients). Why did one lose progress?[/B]
The game keeps one saved-data file per addon for the whole PC. Each client reads it at login and writes its own copy back at logout or /reloadui, so the client that saves last wins. This happens to every addon that saves data across accounts. Let one client log out before the other logs in, or log a character in again on its own afterwards.

[B]Where is my data kept?[/B]
Account-wide in the game's SavedVariables folder, separate for NA and EU. Nothing leaves your PC.

[B]Why are names on the board in English?[/B]
Matching works on any client language; the labels on the board are written in English.
