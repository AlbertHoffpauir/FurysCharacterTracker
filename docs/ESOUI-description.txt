[CENTER][SIZE="6"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Fury's Character Tracker[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[SIZE="3"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][I]Every character on your account, on one board.[/I][/COLOR][/SIZE][/CENTER]
[CENTER][SIZE="1"][COLOR="#B4B0D8"]FURY'S ADDONS[/COLOR]   [URL="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info5063.html"]Guild Management Toolset[/URL]   |   [COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Character Tracker[/B][/COLOR]   |   [URL="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info5099.html"]Inventory Ledger[/URL][/SIZE][/CENTER]

[B]Your whole roster at a glance.[/B] Skill lines, guild ranks, crafting, research, scribing, motifs, recipes, furnishing plans, companions, riding and time played, read straight from the game for every character you have. Note each character's role and gear, put your best numbers on a brag card, and take it all to Excel.
[QUOTE][SIZE="3"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Get your data out of the game[/B][/COLOR][/SIZE]
ESO shows each character only while you are logged in on it, and gives you no way to take the numbers with you. Here, [B]Export to Excel[/B] puts every character's skills, crafting, research, collections and wallet into one CSV, and the [B]offline viewer[/B] shows the whole board in your web browser with the game closed (both in the free Windows Tools).[/QUOTE]
[LIST]
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Alerts you control.[/B][/COLOR]  The only chat lines it writes on its own are the research and riding alerts and a one-line summary at login. They start on, and each one turns off with one click in the settings.
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Read only.[/B][/COLOR]  It reads what the game shows and never changes a character. Roles and gear are your own notes; it never reads what a character is wearing.
[*][COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Any client language.[/B][/COLOR]  Skill lines and companion quests are matched by the game's language-neutral IDs.
[/LIST]
[B]Requires:[/B] [URL="https://www.esoui.com/downloads/info7-LibAddonMenu.html"]LibAddonMenu-2.0[/URL] (version 43 or newer). Install it separately, from the link or by searching for LibAddonMenu in Minion. Without it, this addon will not load.

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Getting started[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*]Install [B]LibAddonMenu-2.0[/B] (see Requires above).
[*]Log in on each character once with the addon on. It reads the character a few seconds after login, and again whenever you make progress (rank up, trait researched, recipe learned, riding lesson, new companion).
[*]Type [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct[/B][/FONT] (or bind a key under Controls > Fury's Character Tracker) to open the board. [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct todo[/B][/FONT] lists characters you haven't logged in on yet.
[*]Optional: turn off any alert you don't want under [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct settings[/B][/FONT], and get the Windows Tools for the offline viewer and the Excel export.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]The board[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I]One row per character, one view per subject.[/I][/COLOR]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Views[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]Overview, Weapons, Armor, Guilds & AvA, World, Crafting, Research, Daily, Collections, Wallet & CP, Roles & gear.
[*][FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct weapons[/B][/FONT] (or any view name; [FONT="Courier New"][B]traits[/B][/FONT] for Research, [FONT="Courier New"][B]sets[/B][/FONT] for Roles & gear) opens straight to it.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Reading it[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*][B]Violet tile[/B] = maxed, white text = in progress, dim dash = not started. Easy to read by brightness alone, not just color.
[*]A [B]Maxed on[/B] row at the bottom counts how many characters have each column maxed.
[*]Hover any cell for details. Click a column title to sort, again to reverse, a third time for your own order.
[*]Click a character for a details window: picks, progress, every skill line, research with timers and every missing trait.
[*]Right-click a row to remove a deleted character. [B]Show removed[/B] brings it back.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Roles & gear[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]Click a blue cell for a dropdown: Role (Tank, Healer, DPS, any two, or all three), DPS gear and Tank/healer gear (Trial set, Crafted set, Overland set, Mixed, None), and a planned race change.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Research and timers[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]Traits known per craft, research slots busy right now, and the time until the next research finishes. Hover a craft for what is researching and every missing trait per item.
[*][B]Research timers[/B] (button on the board, [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct timers[/B][/FONT], or a key): every research in progress, one line each, soonest first. Finished and idle slots are listed at the top in gold.
[*]Timers count down live for every character, not just the one you are on.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Daily, Wallet & CP[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*][B]Daily:[/B] each character's crafting writs since today's reset (started, ready to deliver, done) and when it can take its next riding lesson. Writs reset at 10:00 UTC on NA and 03:00 UTC on EU.
[*][B]Wallet & CP:[/B] gold, Tel Var, Alliance Points, writ vouchers and bag space per character with totals, the bank and transmute crystals, and Champion Points spent per tree.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Alerts[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]A chat line when research or a riding lesson becomes ready on any character, even ones on other accounts, and a one-line summary at login. Turn each on or off in the settings, or with [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct alerts on[/B][/FONT] | [FONT="Courier New"][B]off[/B][/FONT].
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Other accounts and megaservers[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]The button at the top right picks which account and megaserver the board and brag card show. Every account that plays on the same PC saves into the same file, so each one shows up after it logs in once with the addon on. Another account shows what it saved at its last logout.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Brag card[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I][FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct brag[/B][/FONT][/I][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*]One screen of your best numbers, sized for a screenshot to share on Discord: hours played, Champion Points, skill points, skill lines maxed, traits researched, Grandmaster crafters, Vampire + Werewolf, scribing, companions, riding, and the character with the most achievement points, recipes, furnishing plans and motif styles.
[*]Set items collected (sticker book) and outfit styles too. Tiles with a finish line show % complete and a progress bar.
[*]A strip at the bottom shows which roles you play on every class.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Windows Tools[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[COLOR="#B4B0D8"][I]Optional, separate download. The addon works fully in game without them.[/I][/COLOR]
The offline viewer and the Excel export run outside the game, so ESOUI's rules keep them out of the addon zip. Get them here: [B][URL="https://github.com/AlbertHoffpauir/FurysCharacterTracker/raw/main/downloads/FurysCharacterTracker-WindowsTools-1.4.1.zip"]Fury's Character Tracker Windows Tools[/URL][/B], and unzip them into any folder you like, for example Documents\Elder Scrolls Online. In game, the [B]Get the Windows Tools[/B] button under [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct settings[/B][/FONT] (or [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct tools[/B][/FONT]) opens the same download page in your browser.
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Offline viewer[/B][/COLOR]
Everything the addon has saved, for every account and megaserver on your PC, in your web browser with the game closed. Same board as in game: tabs, columns, account button, brag card, research timers and character details.
[LIST=1]
[*]Log out, or type [FONT="Courier New"][B]/reloadui[/B][/FONT]. The game only writes its saved data at those moments.
[*]Double-click [B]Open Offline Viewer.cmd[/B] in the tools folder. Your browser opens [B]Fury's Character Tracker.html[/B], which it also saves in Documents\Elder Scrolls Online.
[*]To see newer data later, run it again. Picks are read-only in the viewer and the in-game-only buttons are grayed out.
[/LIST]
[COLOR="#A08CFF"][B]Export to Excel[/B][/COLOR]
[LIST]
[*][B]One-time setup:[/B] double-click [B]Turn On Auto Export.cmd[/B] in the tools folder. A small helper runs in the background (no admin rights needed).
[*][B]Then, any time:[/B] click [B]Export to Excel[/B] on the board (or [FONT="Courier New"][B]/fct export[/B][/FONT]). The UI reloads, and a few seconds later [B]ESO Characters.csv[/B] is in Documents\Elder Scrolls Online. If it is open in Excel, you get a dated copy beside it.
[*][B]Turn Off Auto Export.cmd[/B] stops the helper; run it before you delete the tools folder. Without the helper, click Export to Excel, then run [B]ExportToExcel.cmd[/B] yourself.
[*][B]Coming from 1.2.1 with Auto Export on?[/B] The scripts used to sit in the addon folder. Download the tools and run [B]Turn On Auto Export.cmd[/B] from there once.
[/LIST]
[B]What each tool file does[/B]
[SPOILER]
All files are plain text (batch, PowerShell, HTML) you can open in Notepad. Full source: [URL="https://github.com/AlbertHoffpauir/FurysCharacterTracker/tree/main/WindowsTools"]WindowsTools on GitHub[/URL]. They only read the addon's saved data file (Documents\Elder Scrolls Online\live\SavedVariables\FurysCharacterTracker.lua) and never connect to the internet or touch the game's files.
[LIST]
[*][B]Open Offline Viewer.cmd / OfflineViewer.ps1:[/B] puts the saved data into a copy of Viewer\viewer.html, saves it as Fury's Character Tracker.html in Documents\Elder Scrolls Online and opens it.
[*][B]Viewer\viewer.html:[/B] the page template (HTML and JavaScript, loads nothing from the internet).
[*][B]ExportToExcel.cmd / ExportToExcel.ps1:[/B] turns the addon's export lines into a CSV in Documents\Elder Scrolls Online.
[*][B]Turn On Auto Export.cmd:[/B] adds one small .vbs to your Windows Startup folder that starts AutoExport.ps1 hidden at sign-in, and starts it now. [B]Turn Off Auto Export.cmd[/B] removes it and stops the helper.
[*][B]AutoExport.ps1:[/B] every 3 seconds checks whether the saved data file changed; when it holds a new export request, runs ExportToExcel.ps1. Keeps a small log and state file in the tools folder.
[/LIST]
[/SPOILER]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Notes on the numbers[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Skill points[/B] are counted the way the game counts them: subclass skills cost double. Max 586.
[*][B]Vampire / Werewolf:[/B] the game hides these lines when you are cured, so the board keeps the best rank it has ever seen.
[*][B]Companions:[/B] the collectible is account-wide, but each character has to finish the companion's intro quest. A companion counts once its intro quest is done on that character, or once you've summoned it there.
[*][B]Scribing[/B] is grimoires + scripts known. [B]Motifs[/B] are styles known in full, the game's own count. [B]Recipes[/B] and [B]furnishing plans[/B] use the game's own lists.
[*]A character only updates when you log in on it.
[/LIST]

[SIZE="4"][FONT="Georgia"][COLOR="#5CC2FF"][B]Questions[/B][/COLOR][/FONT][/SIZE]
[B]I play two accounts at once (two game clients). Why did one lose progress?[/B]
The game keeps one saved-data file per addon for the whole PC. Each client reads it at login and writes its own copy back at logout or /reloadui, so the client that saves last wins. This happens to every addon that saves data across accounts. Let one client log out before the other logs in, or log a character in again on its own afterwards.

[B]Where is my data kept?[/B]
Account-wide in the game's SavedVariables folder, separate for NA and EU. Nothing leaves your PC.

[B]Why are names on the board in English?[/B]
Matching works on any client language; the labels on the board are written in English.
